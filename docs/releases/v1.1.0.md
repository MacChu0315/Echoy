# Echoy v1.1.0

This version improves three-device cooperation, local feedback after recording,
and the center audition area. The source is merged into `master`.
Download the tested firmware, checksums, installation instructions and license
materials from the [v1.1.0 release](https://github.com/MacChu0315/Echoy/releases/tag/v1.1.0).

## Changes

- Join up to three Mosaicos horizontally in the same orientation. Shared read-only
  Flash calibration supports joined startup without per-boot baseline learning.
- Confirm complete neighbor order before starting cooperation, including fast
  reorder. Physical release ends following while preserving the current phrase
  and each tile's playing/paused permission.
- With peers paused, the recording tile plays its prepared reply before group
  activation. Recorded leading rests remain; synchronization cannot replay the
  reply or start continuous playback on a paused peer.
- The guide introduces one tile, then another Echoy tile to its right, and states
  the three-tile maximum. Firmware and About display 1.1.0.
- Center audition draws authentic sound/pitch fragments from the selected role.
  The first response is 350 ms; subsequent lengths follow 0.8 × tap interval, bounded
  to 80–1200 ms. Fast taps also stop by the next onset. Pitch is unchanged.
- Audition accepts at most 10 inputs/s. Excess inputs do not interrupt the current
  fragment, advance random state or queue a later sound. New accepted taps fade
  previous long previews over 16 ms.
- New-role audition waits for the selected role, committed plan and visible image
  to match. A queued tap cannot unexpectedly sound a different role.
- Recording bypasses audition limits and retains count-in, four-beat capture and
  one-shot reply. Office voicing and per-device Play/Pause remain unchanged.

## Validation

The implementation passed:

- `sh tests/band/run.sh`: musical playback, recording and audition regressions.
- `sh tests/render_active/run.sh`: 96 bit-identical scheduler comparisons.
- `sh tests/group/run_three.sh`: all six orders and two join directions.
- `sh tests/group/run_commit_pressure.sh`: 10 commit-pressure cases.
- Horizontal contact/radio and adapter regressions.
- `python -m unittest discover -s tests/device_runtime -v`: 51 runtime tests.
- `python tools/check_ui_responsiveness.py`: real callbacks and held background
  drawing, including the role-change input guard.
- Band/UI address, undefined-behavior and leak checks.
- `python tools/check_three_chain.py`: 10 official native three-owner checks.

Across the repair series, hardware verification covered three-device following
and targeted fast reorder. The final package was checked for same-package healthy
startup, retained layout and audition listening. Detailed
per-device snapshots and experiment logs are retained privately. Historical
measurements are not presented as benchmarks for this release. Sustained RF/load,
all physical arrangements and exhaustive acoustic qualification remain outside
this targeted verification. A previously observed transient startup transaction
failure recovered automatically; its precise cause remains open.
