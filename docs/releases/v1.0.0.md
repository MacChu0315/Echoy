## Contents

- Application, partition information and UI resources in the IRIS bundle.
- No bootloader or Recovery replacement, no later experimental three-device fixes.
- Retained office sound profile and horizontal chains of up to three same-facing
  Mosaico devices, with independent continuous-play permission.
- `INSTALL.md`, `LICENSE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`,
  `echoy-v1.0.0-licenses.zip` and `SHA256SUMS.txt` accompany the download.

The existing source tag predates the new license file. The MIT grant published
on `master` and alongside the firmware explicitly covers the author's original
contributions in that tag; third-party components retain their original terms.
No source tag is moved or recreated for this release.

## What was accepted, and what was not

The initial right-attachment timeout was fixed and the user confirmed stable
following. The user then reported basic three-device operation working. This is
user feedback within those checks, not acceptance of all combinations or timing.

Changing **A—B—C → B—C—A** while running can leave the moved A independent.
Reattaching it did not reliably resolve the recorded case. Startup requires
separation of at least 15 cm on a nonmetal surface for magnetic calibration.
No joined-start support from later versions is included.

The original build, simulator, identity, installation and output-counter checks
are historical evidence. Public README/license packaging did not rerun firmware
builds, install to devices or acquire new acoustic acceptance. Sustained RF load,
full three-device transitions and measured speaker timing remain unaccepted.
Retained Recovery 0.1.2 sometimes reset during its own startup in recorded
updates; installation proceeded only after stable Recovery readiness. This
application does not repair Recovery. Consult the official tool guidance before
changing base firmware.
